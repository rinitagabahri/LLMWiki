- Note: "Why is it worth trusting someone?" The classic two-suspects game: each can stay silent (cooperate) or betray (defect). Payoff ordering T>R>P>S — whatever the other does, defecting dominates, so both defect and both land worse (2 yrs each) than mutual cooperation (1 yr each): individually rational, collectively worse.
