# The Fashion Model

**How you dress is a signal, conscious or not — mapping your style reveals what you're communicating.**

*Model by Eric Sommier.*

## The Matrix

|  | **Old School** | **Hip** |
|---|---|---|
| **Formal** | Classic tie · Double-breasted suit · Oxford shoes · Pocket square · Pearl necklace | Skinny tie · Slim-fit suit · Chelsea boots · Statement glasses · Bold print shirt |
| **Casual** | Woolen tie · Chinos · Loafers · Polo shirt · Trench coat | Sneakers · Hoodie · Ripped jeans · Cap · Oversized jacket |

## What Each Zone Says About You

| Zone | Signal you send |
|---|---|
| Formal + Old School | Reliability, authority, tradition — you respect convention |
| Formal + Hip | Ambition, modernity, controlled edge — you play by the rules but on your own terms |
| Casual + Old School | Comfort, understated confidence — you don't need to prove anything |
| Casual + Hip | Creativity, informality, cultural awareness — you follow the street, not the boardroom |

Most people dress in one zone by default. The interesting question is whether that zone matches the context — and whether it matches who you want to be.

## When to Use It

- Before an important meeting, interview, or event — are you dressed for the signal you want to send?
- When you feel out of place somewhere — your zone may be mismatched to the room
- To understand others quickly — style is a visible, intentional (or unintentional) statement
- To notice if your default dress code has become a habit rather than a choice

## Key Insight

You are always dressed for something — even if you think you're not. Choosing to wear a hoodie to a board meeting is as much a statement as wearing a suit. The model doesn't prescribe a zone; it makes you aware that you're always in one, and asks whether it's intentional.

## Example

*Mateo shows up to a job interview at a creative tech startup in a classic blazer, Oxford shirt, and formal trousers — Formal + Old School. The team is all hoodies and Chelsea boots. She's qualified, but the signal she sends before she speaks a word is: "I don't know your culture." A Formal + Hip choice — slim blazer, no tie, clean sneakers — would have sent the same competence signal with cultural awareness added.*
