# Project Portfolio Matrix

**Evaluate projects by how much they advance your vision AND how much you learn from them.**

|  | **High Learning** | **Low Learning** |
|---|---|---|
| **Aligns with Vision** | 🎯 **Jackpot** — prioritize this | 🤝 **Delegate** — find someone else to do it |
| **Doesn't Align with Vision** | 🔄 **Reshape** — try to change the project | 🗑️ **Reject** — say no |

- **X-axis:** How much does this project help me achieve my overriding objective?
- **Y-axis:** How much am I learning from this project?

## When to Use It

- You're offered a new project and need to decide whether to take it on
- You're reviewing your current portfolio and feel some projects are dead weight
- You're learning a lot but feel scattered — is it moving you toward your goal?
- You want to decide what to delegate vs. what to protect for yourself

## Key Insight

The magic quadrant is high-learning + high-vision-alignment — that's where personal growth and strategic progress compound together. But the sneaky trap is the "delegate" quadrant: projects that serve your vision but teach you nothing new. These feel productive but they're actually maintenance — find someone else to do them so you can stay in the jackpot zone. The worst quadrant (low learning, no vision alignment) should be rejected outright — no guilt needed.

## Example

### Vision = Become CEO

| Project/Opportunity | Quadrant | Reasoning | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leading a new product launch end-to-end | 🎯 Jackpot | Learning general management + gives P&L ownership experience | Protect this — say yes |
| Cross-functional transformation project | 🎯 Jackpot | Learning how other depts work + visible to senior leadership | Prioritize ruthlessly |
| Running operational processes you've already mastered | 🤝 Delegate | Keeps you visible but teaches nothing new | Hand to a direct report who'd grow from it |
| 6th product launch (same playbook) | 🤝 Delegate | Track record is built, no new learning | Let someone else lead; you sponsor |
| Deep technical coding project | 🔄 Reshape | Learning a lot, but pulling away from strategic/leadership work | Reshape into "leading the technical strategy" — delegate execution |
| Administrative committee memberships | 🗑️ Reject | No learning, no CEO-path relevance | Say no politely |
| Legacy maintenance favors | 🗑️ Reject | Neither growth nor vision | Decline or redirect |

**The filter question:** *"Is this teaching me what a future CEO needs to know, AND is it building my track record toward that role?"*

- Yes to both → 🎯 Jackpot
- Builds track record but I'm coasting → 🤝 Delegate
- Fascinating learning but off-path → 🔄 Reshape or timebox
- Neither → 🗑️ Reject
