# SWOT Analysis

**Map your position before making a big decision.**

| | **Helpful** | **Harmful** |
|---|---|---|
| **Internal** (you control) | 💪 Strengths | ⚠️ Weaknesses |
| **External** (you don't) | 🌟 Opportunities | 🔥 Threats |

## When to Use It

- Before a major decision (career, founding something, big investment)
- When you feel stuck and need honest clarity on where you stand
- Annual/quarterly personal review

## Key Insight

The power is in the **combinations**, not the quadrants alone. Ask: "Given this weakness AND this threat, what should I do?" The real insight emerges at the intersections.

## Example

### "Continue in corporate or found my own company?"

*Context: An expat with German citizenship, 10+ years in the IT industry, working in corporate. Deciding whether to stay the course or start something of his own.*

**Option A: Stay in Corporate**

| | **Helpful** | **Harmful** |
|---|---|---|
| **Internal** | 10+ years of IT expertise, stable income, strong professional network, deep knowledge of enterprise systems | Comfort zone limits growth, no ownership of outcomes, career ceiling approaching, struggling with German language limits visibility in German-speaking organizations |
| **External** | German labor law protections, stable economy, employer-funded training, clear promotion path | Someone else sets priorities, corporate restructuring risk, industry moving fast — risk of becoming a "legacy expert" |

**Option B: Found Your Own Company**

| | **Helpful** | **Harmful** |
|---|---|---|
| **Internal** | 10+ years of domain knowledge, technical skills to build an MVP, savings buffer, German citizenship (can freely found a company, access all social systems as fallback) | Completely out of comfort zone — founding is new and hard, no sales/marketing experience, risk-averse after years of corporate safety, no clear product idea yet — has some ideas but doesn't know the greatest customer pain point |
| **External** | AI tools lower cost to build and test ideas, German startup grants (EXIST, KfW), EU market access, growing demand for IT solutions | High uncertainty in the startup world, funded competitors already in the space, economic downturns hit startups first, family/social pressure expects the "safe" path, without a validated pain point — risk of building something nobody wants |

**The intersection questions:**
- *Weakness + Threat:* "I don't know what customers truly need AND the startup world is uncertain. Starting without validation = burning savings on guesses."
- *Weakness + Opportunity:* "I have no clear product yet, BUT AI tools make it cheap to prototype and test multiple ideas fast. Can I validate before I commit?"
- *Strength + Opportunity:* "10 years in IT means I've seen problems up close + AI tools let me build MVPs quickly = I could test ideas on the side while still employed."
- *Strength + Threat:* "German citizenship means I have a safety net (ALG I, healthcare) even if it fails. Does that reduce the fear enough to run experiments?"

**The honest question:** The real blocker isn't "should I leave corporate?" — it's "I don't yet know what to build." The SWOT reveals the next step isn't quitting — it's **validating a pain point while still employed.** Leave corporate only after you know what customers actually need.

## Related Models

- [[eisenhower-matrix]] — Eisenhower is tactical (daily tasks); SWOT is strategic (which direction to go)
- [[appreciative-inquiry]] — SWOT weighs all four quadrants; Appreciative Inquiry works only the Strengths, building forward from what already works
- [[gap-in-the-market-model]] — SWOT names an "Opportunity" quadrant but not how to find one; the Gap model is the tool that locates the external opening — and it answers this page's own unresolved founder question ("I don't yet know what to build") with the same discipline: validate demand before you bet
- [[bcg-box]] — SWOT evaluates one thing deeply; BCG compares multiple bets against each other
- [[crossroads-model]] — they complement each other: SWOT maps your external strategic position, Crossroads your internal compass
- [[strategic-self-assessment]] — theme: SWOT is the position-mapping step of the honest "where do I stand?" sequence
