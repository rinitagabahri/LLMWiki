# The Decision Book Wiki

A personal knowledge base built around **"The Decision Book: Fifty models for strategic thinking"** by Mikael Krogerus and Roman Tschäppeler.

---

## About the Book

The Decision Book presents 50 models for strategic thinking, organized into four sections:

1. **How to Improve Yourself** — models for personal development, productivity, and growth
2. **How to Understand Yourself Better** — models for self-knowledge, reflection, and awareness
3. **How to Understand Others Better** — models for communication, relationships, and group dynamics
4. **How to Improve Others** — models for developing people and turning groups into teams

---

## How This Wiki Works

- Each model gets its own page with a summary, how-to, key insights, and personal notes
- **Theme pages** connect models that share common threads (e.g., [[prioritization]], [[strategic-self-assessment]])
- **Comparison pages** explore relationships between models
- Everything is interlinked — follow the `[[links]]` to explore connections
- The wiki grows with every chapter read
