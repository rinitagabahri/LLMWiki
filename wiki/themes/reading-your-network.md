# Theme: Reading Your Network

Several models treat your relationships not as a private matter but as *readable data* — a map you can inspect, diagnose, and act on. Each reads a different layer of the same network.

## Models in This Theme

| Model | What it reads | The question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| [[family-tree-model]] | **Structure** | How is my network shaped — where's the concentration risk, which boughs could break? |
| [[network-target-model]] | **Composition** | Who's in my circle — is it a mirror of me, or genuinely diverse? |
| [[subtle-signals-model]] | **Communication** | Who do I actually talk to, how often, and to what effect? |
| [[small-world-model]] | **Reach** | How far can my network take me — who am I only a few hops from, and through whom? |

## Synthesis

Put together, they move from *shape* → *make-up* → *behaviour* → *reach*:

- **Family Tree** shows the skeleton — how your contacts connect and where you're over-reliant on one root.
- **Network Target** shows the flesh — whether the people are like you (comfortable, limiting) or diverse (challenging, expanding).
- **Subtle Signals** shows the pulse — whether your day-to-day conversations leave you energised or drained.
- **Small World** shows the range — how far the whole thing extends, since your reach runs through your contacts' contacts (and the *weak* ties bridge you furthest).

A healthy network is diverse in composition, resilient in structure, positive in communication, and *far-reaching* — and these compound: the diverse, well-structured network from the first three models is exactly the one that reaches furthest in the fourth. Most people are strong on one and blind to the rest — which is why mapping all four is more revealing than trusting your gut about "having good relationships."
